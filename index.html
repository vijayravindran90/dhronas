Our vision

Every learner deserves a guide. Every team deserves clarity.

Named after Drona, the legendary teacher of archery, Dhronas helps people and teams take clear aim. We make preparation personal, measurable and affordable, so talent and good engineering are never held back by guesswork.

Personal

Feedback shaped around your resume, your target role, or your own test suite. Never generic advice.

Measurable

Scores, gaps and trends you can track week over week, so progress is something you can see.

Accessible

Generous free tiers and pricing designed for students and small teams, starting at ₹0.

Problems we solve

Preparation is usually guesswork.

01

Students walk into placements unprepared

Resumes get filtered by ATS before a human ever reads them, and mock interviews are rare, costly, or unrelated to the actual job description.

Solved by StudentAI
02

Professionals prepare for the wrong interview

Generic question banks don't reflect the company, domain and level being hired for, so candidates can't see where their real gaps are.

Solved by CareerAI
03

Teams can't trust their test suites

Flaky Playwright tests and slow regressions hide inside CI logs, eroding release confidence and burning engineering hours on reruns.

Solved by Test Analytics
